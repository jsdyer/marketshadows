    Securities

    The report dates tell you when corrections were submitted. Each row gives the period of the original trade. A positive adjustment adds shares to that period’s short-traded volume. A negative adjustment removes shares.

        Changes between reports

        Uses the current report and filters from Securities. Each observation joins a unique symbol and listing market. Share-of-trading measures change in percentage points.

        Reading the reports

        Short sale trading

        Activity marked short during a half-month period. The share of traded volume uses total reported trading volume as its denominator.

        Short positions

        Aggregate shares short on a reporting date, with the published change since the prior report. Compare like reports and check the original filing dates.

        Get the original files

        On CIRO’s site, open Markets → Reports, Statistics and Other Information → Short Sale Trading Statistics and Reports. Choose the Summary, Corrections or Consolidated Short Position section and download a report. Keep their original filenames so the dates travel with your exports.
